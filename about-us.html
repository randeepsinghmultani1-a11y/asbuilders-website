About Us

Who We Are

A S Builders is a general contractor specializing in building envelope restoration: masonry, façade repair, waterproofing, roofing and structural work. Our crews work on everything from small repairs to full-building restorations.

We pair hands-on supervision with proven safety practices, so every job site stays organised, compliant and on schedule.

Our Mission

To foster seamless collaboration among all project stakeholders, leveraging collective expertise to restore, preserve and rebuild our community's structures with purpose, integrity and sustainability.

Craftsmanship

Dedicated to the highest standards in every project, ensuring excellence and durability.

Commitment

Committed to timely and efficient project completion, catering to each client's unique needs.

Integrity

Operating with transparency and trust, fostering strong client relationships.
